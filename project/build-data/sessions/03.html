Session 3 (29 Oct 2026): sentient things and better nonsense daemons. Each student gave a short talk on a "sentient thing" (an object people treat as alive, and what a daemon could learn from it), then showed the improved version of their nonsense daemon and got feedback. After this the real project starts: groups and first concepts.

  • Sentient things: the Quran held over a traveller at the door; the piano; Obi, the coconut oracle; a tunjo, a Muisca gold offering; tarot cards; coffee-ground reading; the Divan of Hafez (Mehdi, from 1:29:33); the pendulum; mirrors.
  • Nonsense daemons: a city of daemons that misread famous architects; a piano that plays wrong notes and a 2D/3D escape game; a house on the sleeping Vastu Purusha that you play by sound; the scaffolding game with endings the daemon chooses, and a spider web; Heaven's Gate judging your face by camera; a house that pulls a girl inside; "The Room of Lost Words" played on everyone's phones (Mehdi, from 1:36:46); the river that judges what you throw in; Dargatum the cat.
  • Feedback for everyone: more is not better (stop when the story is told, do not repeat it); keep your own visual style (generic 3D breaks it and makes people talk about the wrong things); make the work clear without a map or an explanation; test it with real people; small surprises and a strong story hold the audience.
  • On Mehdi's game: testing with the group was important and it is an upgrade from last week; the randomness and not knowing who the daemon is create tension, but all parts must run smoothly so the magic is not lost; the Hafez story was great.
  • Next: submit your nonsense-daemon work within a week (a short description, a video is fine, and the link). The exercise is not graded on its own. Groups form and start their first concepts; consultations follow.
