# DTAD Project · Session 2

> **Session 2 (22 Oct 2026): first nonsense daemons.** Students showed the first versions of their nonsense-daemon prototypes (one theme card plus one constraint card, built with a coding agent) and got feedback from the teaching staff. Everyone improves their prototype for the next session on 29 Oct, together with the five-minute talk on a sentient thing.
> - Projects in part 1: a cat steered by a crowd of players (constraint 6, a crowd of daemons); a bride led by a park daemon into a building (theme 6, constraint 7); Heaven's Gate, a door that sends souls to heaven or hell for absurd reasons (theme 3, constraint 13); a river that judges what falls into it using the Egyptian Book of the Dead (theme 7, constraint 8); living scaffolding that outlasts the university building (theme 15, constraint 5); two snow daemons, the Keeper and the Eraser (theme 10, constraint 14); Bach trapped by the Duke, a daemon that remembers only its last visitor (theme 9, constraint 4).
> - Projects in part 2: the room of lost things with a dying daemon (theme 1, constraint 11, Mehdi's "Last Light", from 0:03:44); houses that move and change shape until sunset, with a cuckoo (remote).
> - Advice for everyone: make it understandable while people watch or play it ("show, don't tell"); keep it simple, abstract and fast; experiment freely; let the daemon decide, instead of you steering everything.
> - Next Wednesday: trip to Leipzig (DOK festival and Elif's installation), by regional train.
